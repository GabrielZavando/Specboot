## Requirements
### Requirement: The build agent owns the Mandatory Steps tick

The build agent (`ai-specs/agents/build-agent.md`) SHALL tick each Mandatory Steps checkbox in `tasks.md` via the edit tool at the moment the step is satisfied (pre-implementation branch/git checks, RED before implementation, module tests during iteration, verify and adversarial-review post-implementation), so `/apply` finishes with zero open Mandatory Steps checkboxes.

#### Scenario: apply ticks steps as they are satisfied
- **GIVEN** a change with a `## Mandatory Steps` section in its `tasks.md` being executed via `/apply`
- **WHEN** the build agent satisfies each step
- **THEN** the corresponding checkbox is marked `[x]` via the edit tool at that moment, and `/apply` ends with no open Mandatory Steps checkboxes

### Requirement: archive is defensive, not the canonical tick owner

The `archive` skill SHALL treat `/apply` as the canonical owner of the Mandatory Steps tick. Archive only defensively ticks satisfied leftovers via the edit tool (never `sed -i`) and aborts on genuinely pending implementation tasks. The standard cycle SHALL NOT require the `--yes` flag because of Mandatory Steps checkboxes.

#### Scenario: archive proceeds without --yes in the standard cycle
- **GIVEN** a change whose `/apply` ticked its Mandatory Steps
- **WHEN** `/archive` parses `tasks.md`
- **THEN** no Mandatory Steps `- [ ]` remain and archive proceeds without `--yes`; a satisfied leftover is defensively ticked via the edit tool; a genuinely pending task aborts the archive

### Requirement: The roadmap reflects the executed cycle

`PLAN_MEJORAS_SPECBOOT.md` SHALL mark M-701 as `## [x]`, register M-908 as a completed ticket (`## [x] M-908`) following the M-403 pattern (SemVer level and dependencies declared), and add a history row covering the M-701 + M-908 + M-909 cycle.

#### Scenario: roadmap registers the completed cycle
- **GIVEN** `PLAN_MEJORAS_SPECBOOT.md` with M-701 unmarked and M-908 unregistered
- **WHEN** the change is applied
- **THEN** M-701 is marked `## [x]`, M-908 appears as `## [x] M-908`, and the history row for the cycle exists

### Requirement: The proposal template carries Why and What Changes

The `plan-change` skill proposal template SHALL include `## Why` and `## What Changes` sections, and its Step 6 checklist SHALL validate their presence, so proposals generated by the template do not trigger the `openspec archive` proposal warning.

#### Scenario: template-generated proposal passes without warnings
- **GIVEN** the updated `plan-change` template
- **WHEN** a new change proposal is generated
- **THEN** it includes `## Why` and `## What Changes` and `openspec archive` emits no proposal warning for it
